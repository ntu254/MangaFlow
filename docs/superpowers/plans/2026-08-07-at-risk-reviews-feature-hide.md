# Hide At-risk Reviews Feature From UI Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Remove the "At-risk Reviews" nav entry, close its route (redirect
away instead of rendering), and remove the dashboard widgets that link into
it — frontend-only, no backend change, no source-file deletion.

**Architecture:** All three UI entry points (nav config, route file,
dashboard component) are edited independently; `features/board/at-risk/**`
source stays intact and unreached, so the change is a single revert away
from being undone.

**Tech Stack:** React, TanStack Router, TypeScript, ESLint, `tsc --noEmit`.

## Global Constraints

- Do not modify `backend/**`.
- Do not delete `features/board/at-risk/**`, `entities/board/model/board-types.ts`
  at-risk types, or `useAtRiskDecisionMutation`.
- Do not change risk badges/pills on other pages (`RankingTable`, series
  overview, chapter KPI strip, rankings pages).
- Do not change `board-queue-page.tsx` / `board-voting-queue-page.tsx`
  at-risk filtering or `board-notifications-page.tsx` notification types.
- Do not touch unrelated dirty files (`frontend/src/routeTree.gen.ts` is
  auto-generated by the TanStack Router plugin on dev/build — let it
  regenerate itself, don't hand-edit it).

### Task 2: Close the route

- [ ] **Step 1: Replace the component with a redirect**

`app.board.$id.tsx`: add a `beforeLoad` that throws
`redirect({ to: "/app/board/dashboard" })`, and drop the `component:

